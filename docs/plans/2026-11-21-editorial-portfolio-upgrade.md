## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

Transform the Projects and Certifications sections of the **Lilian Alkhawalda** portfolio from a repetitive, text-heavy grid of white cards into an **Editorial Luxury Portfolio** featuring:
- **10 Fully Audited & Documented Projects** (expanded from 7 with Figma UI/UX system, Flutter mobile app, and Code MAP systems analysis).
- **Direct, clickable, verified links** for every project: GitHub Repositories, Figma Designs & Prototypes, Live Demos, and In-Depth Case Studies.
- **Rich, authentic visual assets**: Real screenshots, Figma master canvas exports, and recorded walk-throughs.
- **Distinguished Certifications Showcase**: Separation of Al-Aqsa University Bachelor's Degree from professional certifications; verified Skilljar / Anthropic verification URLs for all 13 AI certs; TAQAT Academy original PNG certificates; Bright Gaza and LinkedIn verified links; plus an embedded Certificate Viewer modal for instant preview.
- **Editorial Asymmetric UI Layout**: Moving away from monotonous white boxes to a dynamic layout balancing `Soft Ivory`, `Midnight`, and `Pure White` with signature `Rose Pink` and `Berry Rose` accents.
- **Bilingual EN/AR parity** with genuine RTL/LTR typography.
